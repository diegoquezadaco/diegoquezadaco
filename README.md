## About Me
👋 Hi, I'm Diego, a Robotics and Digital Systems Engineering graduate from [Tecnológico de Monterrey](https://www.tec.mx/en), Querétaro, Mexico.

I build robots and learning systems that stay reliable outside the lab — across perception, sensor fusion, autonomous navigation, and simulation-based robotics. Recent work includes a LiDAR-based autonomous navigation stack (Puzzlebot RPLIDAR), a vision-driven autonomous driving system on embedded hardware (Puzzlebot Jetson), and an industrial robotic digital twin in NVIDIA Isaac Sim built to close the sim-to-real gap, developed during a research placement at the University of Victoria (Mitacs Globalink), Canada.

Check out my work on [my portfolio](https://diegoquezadaco.github.io/index.html).
